KORU

A steel-hulled sailing yacht built in the Netherlands by Oceanco as yard number Y721 and delivered in 2023. Dykstra drew the naval architecture and exterior; Mlinaric, Henry & Zervudachi did the interior. Oceanco has not confirmed the owner; the link to Jeff Bezos is publicly reported.

METHODOLOGY

Trust the timestamp,
not the narrative.

01

Public broadcasts

Positions come from public AIS messages received through free data sources.

02

Validation first

Invalid coordinates, timestamp regressions, and physically impossible jumps are rejected.

03

Delayed publishing

The public record trails the collector by 15 minutes and never exposes internal credentials or raw errors.

04

Honest uncertainty

“Signal gap” means the collector is healthy but no verified fix arrived. “Source unavailable” means we cannot tell.
